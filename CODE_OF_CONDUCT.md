# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Tucklane to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

* being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences,
* giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback,
* focusing on what is best for the community and the people who use Tucklane,
* showing empathy and kindness.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

* harassment, insults or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks,
* sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances,
* publishing others' private information without explicit permission,
* other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when someone represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers privately via GitHub (for
example through a private security advisory on this repository, marked
"Code of Conduct"). All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially.
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or
permanently ban contributors whose behavior is harmful.
